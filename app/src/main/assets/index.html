Setup

  1. Enable AOD Enabler in LSPosed Manager.
  2. Scope: System Framework, SystemUI, Settings. Do not tick the AOD Enabler app.
  3. Reboot the device.
  4. Open Settings > Display. The AOD toggle should be there.
